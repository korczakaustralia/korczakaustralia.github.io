International Day of Happiness March 20 🎉

"Let your smile change the world, don't let the world change your smile."
2026 Theme: Caring and Sharing
The theme from 2025 is still in place, and this year with a focus on connection, empathy, and helping others too.
International Happiness Day reminds us that happiness is very important. Happiness is not only important just on this special day. We feel great when we are happy, we often make other people happy too. We laugh a lot, we also play more and learn better too! How great it would be if there was happiness in almost every day!
What can you do to make some happy moments even on tough days? And help others feel happy too?
